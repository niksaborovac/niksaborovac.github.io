About me

Born & raised in a small town in central Bosnia, where I completed primary and secondary education. I first encountered the word "geodesy" in 2015 and from that moment on I knew that it would be my profession.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Geodesy and Geoinformatics from the University of Split and am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Applied Geoinformatics at the University of Salzburg, with a focus on hydrography and offshore applications.
